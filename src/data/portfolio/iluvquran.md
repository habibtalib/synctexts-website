## The Challenge

iLuvQuran is a Malaysian Quran education company with 10 branches and around 5,900 learning-platform users. Its systems had grown piece by piece: a large WordPress website, a paid third-party school-management SaaS, a vendor-built class-booking app, and everything hosted on shared cPanel servers. The company needed one technology direction, systems it owns, and infrastructure that can grow with it.

## Our Approach

SyncTexts founder Habib Talib serves as iLuvQuran's CTO, leading the platform roadmap and the engineering work:

- **Infrastructure move** — migrating the whole estate off cPanel onto Google Cloud, with DNS and edge protection on Cloudflare.
- **Headless website** — rebuilding iluvquran.com from WordPress as an Astro front end with Payload CMS, using an automated content migration with parity checks and unchanged URLs.
- **In-house back office** — a Laravel and Filament intranet that replaces the paid school SaaS: students, teachers, branches, class scheduling, attendance, invoicing, payroll, a sales CRM, event registration and payments, and a guardian portal, with personal data encrypted at rest.
- **QuranHome booking** — an in-house rebuild of the one-to-one online class booking app, with a guided booking wizard and SecurePay and CHIP payments.
- **Live KPIs** — a wall-mounted TV dashboard that rotates operations, growth and executive views from aggregate data only.

## Results

- A clear, owned technology estate in place of rented and vendor-locked tools.
- One back office for branch operations, billing and parents, replacing a recurring SaaS subscription.
- A faster, editor-friendly website and scalable cloud hosting for future growth.
